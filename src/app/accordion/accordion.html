Dragon fruit, also known as Pitaya or Pitahaya, is a tropical fruit that comes from several cactus species. It is recognized by its bright pink or yellow skin with green scales and its sweet, juicy flesh filled with tiny black seeds. Dragon fruit is popular for its refreshing taste, vibrant appearance, and numerous health benefits.

Dragon fruit is rich in Vitamin C, dietary fiber, antioxidants, magnesium, and iron. Regular consumption may help strengthen the immune system, improve digestion, support heart health, and protect the body against harmful free radicals. It is also low in calories, making it a healthy snack for people of all ages.

A serving of dragon fruit contains essential nutrients such as Vitamin C, Vitamin B, calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, and antioxidants. It also contains a high amount of water, which helps keep the body hydrated while providing natural energy throughout the day.
